# Agent Note: Devil's Advocate challenges analysis reports

Status: proposed

English | [中文](2026-10-28-conductor-analysis-critic.zh.md)

## Problem

Analysis currently accepts a producer's report after Auditor and Arbiter without an independent challenge to its reasoning. Auditor checks factual claims and file/line references; that does not establish that priorities, alternatives, risk trade-offs or recommendations follow from the cited material. The owner reports that CON-2026-0687 (CustomAgents) and CON-2026-7660 (biyocon-harness) both reached Arbiter acceptance without a Devil's Advocate critic. Reported role tokens were Detective 303K/378K, Strategist 150K/138K, Auditor 165K/196K and Arbiter 30K/26K, respectively. These are owner-reported observations, not replayed sessions; no private session content is included.

On [biyocon/master 45d316f](https://github.com/Biyocon/biyocon-harness/tree/45d316f852a7ad104375e07a148a48ca67f3b730), [admittedRoute and reportProducer](https://github.com/Biyocon/biyocon-harness/blob/45d316f852a7ad104375e07a148a48ca67f3b730/packages/conductor/conductor/src/intake.ts#L159-L179) still route Detective-entry recommendations through Detective → Strategist → Auditor → Arbiter, and an answer through its entry role → Auditor → Arbiter. Devil's Advocate can produce an entry-C report but does not challenge another report. [driveAnalysis](https://github.com/Biyocon/biyocon-harness/blob/45d316f852a7ad104375e07a148a48ca67f3b730/packages/conductor/conductor/src/analysis.ts#L153) drives those read-only stages. [recordGate](https://github.com/Biyocon/biyocon-harness/blob/45d316f852a7ad104375e07a148a48ca67f3b730/packages/conductor/conductor/src/state.ts#L235) requires an approved execution baseline only for implementation-C pass; [parseAnalysisState](https://github.com/Biyocon/biyocon-harness/blob/45d316f852a7ad104375e07a148a48ca67f3b730/packages/conductor/conductor/src/analysis-persistence.ts#L14) rejects analysis execution authority. The record format is [v4](https://github.com/Biyocon/biyocon-harness/blob/45d316f852a7ad104375e07a148a48ca67f3b730/packages/conductor/conductor/src/state.ts#L35).

## Proposal

Require a Devil's Advocate challenge on every analysis run, with no silent opt-out. Preserve the seven role identities and all required intake/preparation steps. The six admitted combinations have these proposed paths; Gate C records separate `producer` and `critic` occurrences when Devil's Advocate is the entry role. Gate D (Headsman) and Gate F (Integrator) are always skipped in analysis.

| Entry | Deliverable | Producer and required preparation | Critic | Fact check | Decision | Skipped gates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Detective | recommendations | Detective (A) prepares scope; Strategist (B) produces | Devil's Advocate (C) | Auditor (E) | Arbiter (G) | D, F |
| Detective | answer | Detective (A) | Devil's Advocate (C) | Auditor (E) | Arbiter (G) | B, D, F |
| Strategist | recommendations | Strategist (B) | Devil's Advocate (C) | Auditor (E) | Arbiter (G) | A, D, F |
| Strategist | answer | Strategist (B) | Devil's Advocate (C) | Auditor (E) | Arbiter (G) | A, D, F |
| Devil's Advocate | recommendations | Devil's Advocate (C, producer) | Devil's Advocate (C, critic) | Auditor (E) | Arbiter (G) | A, B, D, F |
| Devil's Advocate | answer | Devil's Advocate (C, producer) | Devil's Advocate (C, critic) | Auditor (E) | Arbiter (G) | A, B, D, F |

Both entry-C deliverables use two fresh, distinct Devil's Advocate children. Typed dispatch mode (`producer` or `critic`), distinct child IDs, attempts, prompts, source sessions and stored outputs distinguish them; the critic receives the candidate and referenced material, not the producer's prior transcript. This is separation of work and context, not an assertion that the same role has an independent viewpoint. Arbiter must see that provenance and cannot accept a candidate reviewed only by its own producer child.

The critic precedes Auditor. It asks whether priorities, alternatives, omitted risks, assumptions and recommendations follow from the evidence; it does not recheck every file/line citation. Auditor then verifies facts and exact citations in the final candidate, including any factual premise promoted from the challenge. Every new candidate version, whether caused by Gate C, Auditor or Arbiter rework, requires a new challenge before a fresh audit; Arbiter accepts only the latest exact candidate, matching challenge and matching audit. A citation defect in the candidate uses Auditor's existing Gate E `defect: analysis` rework to the producer, then the new candidate passes through critic and Auditor again. A defect confined to Auditor's own report uses Gate E `defect: report` to retry Auditor against the unchanged candidate and valid challenge. Gate E counts both Auditor rework paths; Gate C counts critic rework. This keeps critique and factual verification distinct and prevents auditing a candidate that critique will immediately replace.

### Challenge result and Gate C

Propose a dedicated `AnalysisChallenge` structured result: `run_id`, `purpose: analysis`, `candidate_hash`, `candidate_version`, ordered `material_ids`, `critic_child_id`, `critic_attempt`, `findings` with stable ID, severity (`minor`/`major`/`critical`), claim, rationale and material IDs, `conclusion: holds|does_not_hold`, and bounded summary. The runtime computes a challenge hash from validated content. The result has no execution baseline, implementation approval or command evidence. A Gate C history record binds mode `critic`, child/attempt, candidate hash, challenge hash and decision. The existing entry-C producer pass is explicitly mode `producer`; neither can impersonate the other. A `holds` result with unresolved major/critical findings is contradictory and rejected.

Validation rejects a foreign run, unknown material ID, stale candidate/version/hash, reused child or attempt, mismatched dispatch mode, duplicate/conflicting challenge, changed finding content under the same hash, out-of-order Gate C and an Arbiter decision without matching candidate/challenge/audit hashes. A malformed result is a report-validation failure at the critic, never a synthetic `holds`. Persist the exact candidate → challenge → audit → Arbiter links before dispatching successors; replay validates the same causal sequence and rejects a partially checkpointed or contradictory record. A crash before the challenge checkpoint cannot turn an unrecorded result into a pass; restart resumes only the durable stage or parks ambiguous dispatch for owner review.

This changes the durable run meaning: propose `CONDUCTOR_RECORD_FORMAT_VERSION = 5`. Every run has a required `analysisChallenges: CapturedChallenge[]` (empty for implementation); each entry stores the validated result, its runtime hash, candidate hash, critic child ID and attempt. Each v5 analysis candidate also stores typed `material_spans` (`material_id`, inclusive `start_line`/`end_line`) for its cited captured materials; the host validates the spans against the admitted material hashes before constructing critic input. Analysis Gate C history requires `mode: producer|critic` and the exact candidate hash; critic records also require the challenge hash and child/attempt. Analysis `ReportDecision` requires `challenge_hash` alongside its candidate/audit hashes. These v5 fields make producer and critic occurrences, replay order and Arbiter acceptance unambiguous. v4 has no challenge provenance and is rejected with the existing unsupported-format error, without migration or compatibility shim. Implementation-only v5 records keep their current authority rules. [#30 phase 2](https://github.com/Biyocon/biyocon-harness/issues/30) must take the next monotonic format version (v6 if it follows v5 directly), never reuse v5 with a different meaning.

### Rework, owner and authority

`does_not_hold` records critic Gate C rework targeted at `reportProducer(admission)`; scope defects return to Detective, plan defects to Strategist, through the existing prerequisite rules. The producer emits a new version/hash, followed by a new critic child and audit. A malformed critic result is the critic's report failure: record Gate C `defect: report` and dispatch a fresh critic child/attempt against the same candidate without rerunning the producer. It consumes one `maxReworkPerGate` Gate C rework, not Gate E or an owner reassessment. The same per-run Gate C budget also counts substantive critic rework and, for entry C, producer rework; it is not reset by a new candidate. With the current configurable default of two reworks, the third Gate C rework request escalates with unresolved findings instead of another child dispatch. Gate A/B/E/G counters remain independent. A foreign/stale identity or authority violation fails closed and escalates immediately. Critical safety or authority findings escalate immediately with the exact reason; a substantive but repairable major finding uses rework. No verdict silently passes a challenge.

Reuse [#37's owner ledger and continuation](../../implemented/feature/2026-10-30-conductor-owner-escalation-resumption.md): exact run, candidate/challenge hash, gate history index, child attempt and required questions are stored in the existing question/answer/consumed/rejected ledger. `maxOwnerReassessmentsPerGate` remains independently bounded. Reuse required `conductor/owner-question-created`, `conductor/owner-response-recorded`, `conductor/owner-response-acknowledged`, `conductor/owner-continuation-consumed` and `conductor/owner-terminal-rejection` events and the existing owner card actions `approve`, `reject`, `answer_continue` (Godkend / Afvis / Svar og fortsæt). `approve` is offered only for a concrete validated proposal; an analysis answer or approved clarification may trigger one read-only reassessment, never execution approval. An owner reply cannot create an execution baseline, authorize implementation, reset Gate C rework, or reach Headsman, Ralph, Integrator or closeout. Implementing recommendations needs a separate implementation run and its own authorization.

### Input, cost and presentation

Give the critic the latest candidate report, its content hash/version, acceptance criteria, the bounded material-reference index and deterministic excerpts. The candidate's typed material spans identify captured material IDs and inclusive line ranges. The runtime, not a model, validates each span against the immutable admission capture and its hash, selects those exact lines, enforces `maxCriticInputTokensPerAttempt`, and logs the exact excerpts and complete model-visible request in the child session before dispatch. Missing, out-of-range or over-budget spans block the critic dispatch instead of silently truncating evidence. Do not replay Detective's repository exploration or full child transcript. The critic has only `structured_output`; no `read`, `read_image`, `grep`, `glob`, write, edit, shell or other file tool. [ToolRestriction](../../../../packages/conductor/tool-conductor/src/capability.ts) filters tool names only, so it cannot enforce path access from material IDs; runtime-owned excerpts avoid that gap. The challenge result and Gate C state are checkpointed, and the logged request plus captured material hashes allow reconstruction of everything the critic saw.

The canonical [Devil's Advocate role instructions](../../../conductor/roles/03-devils-advocate.md) and [conductor-specialist skill](../../../skills/conductor-specialist/SKILL.md) must define a typed analysis `critic` mode alongside the existing `producer` mode. In critic mode the role returns `AnalysisChallenge`: a candidate-bound conclusion, falsifiable reasoning findings, alternatives, omitted risks and material IDs; it does not produce a replacement candidate or audit citations. Both canonical files declare the supported analysis modes in parsed metadata; preset validation checks for `critic` there rather than inferring it from prose. [Source launch](../../../../apps/cli/scripts/source-launch.ts) regenerates the installation skills at each startup under #25/#59. [Preset validation](../../../../packages/conductor/conductor-presets/src/index.ts) must reject a role or specialist skill lacking the critic mode, even if the asset exists, so a stale producer-only instruction cannot mount.

A separate `conductor/child` event already carries role, attempt and gate; extend typed dispatch/stage data with producer/critic mode and exact child binding so [#56](https://github.com/Biyocon/biyocon-harness/issues/56) can show the role rather than the prompt/UUID. This proposal does not implement #56. A fresh child starts with no inherited gate badge. Web UI shows EN/DA text for role, attempt and `holds`/`does_not_hold` or pending/escalated, not color alone.

The owner's two reported runs used 648K and 738K tokens across the four listed roles. Estimate 20–40K additional tokens for one bounded critic attempt, about 3–6% of those totals; two reworks can mean three attempts (60–120K additional critic tokens), excluding producer reruns. Add validated `tool-conductor` Config fields, overridable in `cordis.yml`: `maxCriticInputTokensPerAttempt` (default 24,000), `maxCriticOutputTokensPerAttempt` (default 16,000, including reported reasoning output) and `maxCriticTokensPerRun` (default 120,000, counting all provider-reported input, output and reasoning tokens without double counting). Validate positive safe integers and a run ceiling at least as large as the sum of the configured per-attempt input and output caps; reject invalid preset config at load. Reserve that sum before dispatch, require the provider output cap to include reasoning tokens (otherwise reject that provider for critic dispatch), record actual usage per attempt and escalate to the owner if the remaining run budget cannot admit another attempt. No limit is hardcoded in dispatch logic. Compare actual cost in keyless/provider tests; critique is mandatory from the first enabled analysis run. A future opt-out, if ever introduced, must be explicit and logged per run, never a hidden configuration switch. [#58](https://github.com/Biyocon/biyocon-harness/issues/58) owns Detective cost reduction; this design neither expands Detective input nor authorizes a second whole-repository exploration.

## Alternatives considered

**Critic after Auditor.** It repeats citation checking or invalidates an expensive audit when it demands a new candidate; challenge before audit is cheaper and keeps responsibilities distinct.

**Auditor as sole critic.** Auditor already owns factual and citation verification, so combining reasoning challenge with that pass hides which judgment failed and preserves the current gap.

**Optional or silent critique.** It allows an accepted analysis to bypass the owner's requested role and makes restart semantics dependent on an unrecorded choice. Mandatory critique has a visible token cost and a stable route.

**Treat a second Devil's Advocate child as fully independent at entry C.** A fresh session and separate mode prevent self-review of one child output, but the same role/model is not an independent discipline; provenance must state that limit.

## Risks

The added critic increases latency and tokens, especially after rework; the bounded input, usage ceiling and explicit owner stop prevent silent cost growth but do not reduce Detective's existing cost. Entry-C producer/critic children share a role/model, so the design guarantees separate sessions and evidence binding, not independent expertise. v4 runs cannot resume under v5; pre-release rejection is intentional. No runtime, schema or #30 phase-1 changes are part of this proposed note.
